A Rant about Socioeconomic Deterrents 

Sep 1, 2026

My brain, like everybody's, over-simplifies the world and unless checked turns complex dynamic concepts into black and white binary framings. When I allow myself to go that route and not acknowledge the spectrum with which things lie, I categorize people into one of two groups. Normatively thriving and adversely struggling. In the former group are people who are able to conform to society in ways that enable them to be more resilient and dynamic to the curveballs life throws at people. They usually have a professional job and are able to maintain it. They display self-discipline and self-regulation. They have an internal locus of control. Life doesn't happen to them, they make life happen. They can be motivated by and have the patience for delayed reward. They do not struggle with overuse of substances. They are able to engage in "healthy" relationships, both romantic and platonic. Have healthy attachment styles and are independent. They consider themselves generally happy or content. And the latter group being mostly the opposite. They regularly struggle to cope with the curveballs life throws. Jumping from job to job is normal for them. Emotional regulation is difficult. They have an external locus of control, life happens to them, they perceive minimal autonomy. Immediate gratification is influential in their decision process. Difficulties controlling substance use. Tend to have many partners for short periods and have the unhealthier of the attachment styles and codependent. They would not classify themselves as happy or content. 


Of course, I acknowledge the level of bias is high here, I am over simplifying and making massive generalizations, and I recognize how unscientific this is, especially considering I am literally typing this while I sit in a college research class. Humans are so much more complex than words could ever describe. But personal bias is the point here, this is to expel into writing my own thoughts and understandings, how Austin sees the world. Because I am actually tired of hearing intelligent educated people talk in terms of political correctness and theory and in alignment with the scientific method and research methods. I want to hear how intelligent educated people actually understand the world in their own heads. Unfortunately, it tends to be the undereducated that feel the desire to express their truest thoughts out loud. I have zero interest in hearing that either. 


I have my own hypotheses as to why somebody falls into one group over the other.  Attachment style and all the factors that help to shape which one someone has. Upbringing, the amount of time and energy with which your caregivers gave you as a child. Intentional lesson learning, parents explicitly teaching their children life lessons and giving them a more accurate view of reality. Socioeconomic status of parents, but more so their readiness, were they a planned child or an accidental child, were their parents intentionally trying to conceive because they knew they had the resources to adequately have a child or were resources lacking for their parents when they came into the world. Access. Access to material resources, emotional resources, life opportunities, A large part of what my theory consists of is based around institutional socioeconomic status and systemic oppression. My theory has nothing to do with the person themselves. I do not believe they are "lazy" or disabled in some way or any other reason that may fall on the individual but rather I see people largely as products of their environments. 


I could go on and on and on but all this to say that life is extremely unfair and while fighting for the concept of equality and equity in a political sense is an action I absolutely support, the concepts in terms of philosophy are a fucking joke. There is no such thing as equal or fair life is inherently NOT that way, but that doesn't mean we shouldn't still strive to reach it.
