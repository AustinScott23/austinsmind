Chronic Existential Crisis

Sometimes it boggles my mind how people can just go about their day as if we aren't all on a huge flying ball circling an even larger ball of fire in a vast unending space of literal nothingness. Nobody knows why we are here or if there is a goal or a purpose. Many people have convinced themselves or been convinced by others that they know the true purpose of why we must experience this crazy thing called life. We usually label it "religion" when lots of people come together claiming to know the same purpose. Many people give themselves one, one that is only contextually relevant to them and their lives. But at the end of the day nobody knows anything. No instructions were handed out before becoming a human body. No introduction class, no tutorial, no guide, no objective. And how did this thing called life even come into being to begin with? Well the smartest and most educated people on the entire planet whose job it is to find the answer to things through something we call science, well they say it just appeared. Out of literal nothingness. They can explain some of the most abstract and complex things on the planet yet when asked how the planet came to be, they answer, "it kind of just appeared is our best guess." I am constantly asking myself questions of this nature and it boggles my mind that others aren't.
